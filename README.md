## The Moon Finally Answers Back

One line, 1 to 240 characters, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to the wishes, howls and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it. A judge model reads the lines two at a time and keeps the funnier one, round after round, until the funniest line takes the largest share of the reward.

This entry is a single line in the Moon's own voice: old, wry, and tired of being wished upon. It answers the wishes, the howls, and the 2 a.m. promises to change in one breath, then turns on the reader — it is a rock, not a genie, and no, he is not texting you back.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`): one line of plain text, 171 characters.
